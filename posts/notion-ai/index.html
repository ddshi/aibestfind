Deep Dive: Key Features That Impressed Us

1. AI Writing That Feels Native

The core feature is the AI writing assistant. You can trigger it by pressing the spacebar or clicking the AI icon in the toolbar. From there, you can ask it to draft a blog post, write a project proposal, or even generate a haiku about your quarterly goals. The output quality is surprisingly good ‚Ä?especially for business writing.

We tested it by asking for a 500-word product launch plan. The AI generated a structured outline with sections for timeline, budget, marketing channels, and success metrics. It wasn't perfect, but it saved us roughly 20 minutes of formatting and brainstorming. The key advantage? It lives inside your Notion page, so you can edit, expand, or reorganize without switching tabs.

2. Summarization: Condense Without Losing Context

One of the most practical uses is the summarize feature. Highlight a long page, click "Summarize," and Notion AI returns a concise bullet-point version. We tested it on a 3,000-word meeting notes page from a product team. The summary captured all six major decisions, three action items, and two risks ‚Ä?missing only one minor detail about a vendor contract.

This feature is a game-changer for anyone who deals with lengthy documentation. As one beta tester put it: "I used to spend 10 minutes scanning notes before standups. Now I just hit summarize and get the key points in seconds."

3. Q&A: Your Workspace as a Knowledge Base

The Ask AI feature lets you query your entire workspace. Type something like "What were the key takeaways from last week's design review?" and Notion AI searches through all your pages, databases, and comments to provide an answer. It can even pull from linked documents.

In our tests, it correctly answered 8 out of 10 questions about a complex project with 15+ interconnected pages. The two failures were due to ambiguous phrasing ‚Ä?when we rephrased the questions, it worked. This is still a beta-level feature, but it's already incredibly useful for teams with large knowledge bases.
