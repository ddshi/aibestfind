2. Custom Style Training

KREA AI allows you to train a custom style model using your own images. This is a game-changer for brands or artists who want a consistent visual identity. You upload 10— 0 images (e.g., your product photos, a specific art style), and KREA trains a lightweight model in about 15 minutes. Once trained, you can generate new images in that exact style. We tested this with a set of watercolor paintings, and the results were remarkably faithful — colors, brush texture, and composition all matched the original set. 3. AI Upscaler and Image Enhancement

KREA AI includes a built-in upscaler that can increase image resolution by up to 4x without losing detail. We tested it on a 512x512 generated image, and the 2048x2048 result retained sharp edges and fine textures — no blurry artifacts. Additionally, the "Enhance" feature can improve lighting, contrast, and sharpness with a single click. For content creators who need high-res assets for print or social media, this is a practical time-saver.

4. Inpainting and Outpainting

Need to remove an object or extend the background? KREA AI's inpainting tool lets you select an area and regenerate it with a new prompt. Outpainting extends the canvas beyond the original borders, filling in new content that matches the existing style. We found both features to be intuitive and responsive, though outpainting sometimes produces slightly less coherent results on complex scenes — a common limitation of current AI models.
