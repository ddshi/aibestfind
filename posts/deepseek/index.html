The Tradeoffs (And They're Real)

1. Creative Writing Falls Short

This is where DeepSeek noticeably falls behind. Its writing voice is functional and clinical. It gets facts right but lacks personality. When I asked all three AIs to write a blog post about AI trends, ChatGPT's version was engaging, Claude's was polished and articulate, and DeepSeek's was accurate but dry �?like a well-written textbook rather than a magazine article.

2. Content Restrictions

DeepSeek is developed by a Chinese company, and like all Chinese AI platforms, it has content restrictions. Questions about politically sensitive topics are declined. For most professional use cases this doesn't matter, but be aware of it.

3. Server Congestion

During peak hours (Asia daytime), DeepSeek sometimes throws "server busy" errors. In my 30-day test, this happened 4 times �?not terrible, but annoying when you're in the middle of work.
