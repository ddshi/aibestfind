AI News Digest: August 12, 2026

Today’s AI landscape is defined by a powerful tension between explosive commercial growth and mounting ethical friction. Venture capital continues to flood into the sector, with AI coding startups like Cognition and Lovable achieving astronomical valuations, while established players like Google and OpenAI push their consumer products to unprecedented scale. Science & Research

Scientists Just Created Female Clones of Male Mice

In a groundbreaking scientific achievement, researchers have successfully created female clones from the cells of male mice. The technique involved a complex process of genetic engineering to replace the Y chromosome with an X chromosome, effectively reprogramming the cell’s sex.

While the resulting mice were viable, they experienced a shortened lifespan, indicating that the imprinting of sex chromosomes remains a complex biological hurdle. This research opens up new possibilities for reproductive biology and genetic engineering, though its application to higher mammals and humans remains a distant and ethically fraught prospect.
