1. What ClickUp’s Mass Layoff Tells Us About the Future of Work

Key Insights: Productivity software company ClickUp laid off a significant portion of its workforce, a move that analysts interpret as a canary in the coal mine for the future of work. The layoffs are not just a cost-cutting measure but a strategic pivot, suggesting that the company believes AI can now automate roles previously filled by humans, particularly in customer support and sales. This event underscores a brutal reality: the first wave of AI-driven job displacement may not be in creative fields, but in the very SaaS tools designed to make us more productive.

By focusing on the human soul rather than machine logic, the document reframes the AI conversation from one of capability to one of ethical imperative, challenging technologists to consider the societal fallout of their creations.
