Today's AI landscape is dominated by a governance crisis at the world's most valuable chipmaker. The Nvidia Crisis

1. Nvidia's Stock Drops as Board Reportedly Preps to Oust CEO Jensen Huang

The biggest story in tech today: Nvidia's board is reportedly preparing to remove CEO Jensen Huang, and investors reacted swiftly, driving the stock down. The move would mark one of the most dramatic corporate leadership upheavals in Silicon Valley history, given Huang's two-decade tenure as the architect of the AI hardware boom. The board's apparent urgency signals that directors view the situation as an existential governance threat rather than a manageable reputational issue.

  • The reported ouster comes amid a fast-moving scandal that has consumed Nvidia's leadership in a matter of days.
  • Markets are pricing in significant uncertainty over Nvidia's strategic direction and customer relationships.
