Today in AI: Model Wars Heat Up as Safety Shakeups and Agent Mania Define the Week

Today's AI landscape is defined by tectonic shifts on multiple fronts. Google's release of Gemini 4 Argon — billed as its most powerful model yet — signals an intensifying frontier race, while OpenAI's reported dismissal of three safety researchers raises fresh questions about the lab's internal culture. Meanwhile, the agent economy is accelerating: Shopify, DoorDash, and Amazon all unveiled new AI-powered products, and ElevenLabs doubled its valuation to $22 billion. If there's a throughline, it's this: the industry is racing to build autonomous systems while still grappling with who governs them.

The move is likely to fuel criticism that commercial pressure is eroding OpenAI's safety-first founding ethos.

It's a significant blow to the open web ecosystem and could accelerate the fragmentation of online information.

  • Reddit blames AI bots for the decision to cut RSS and API access
