Prince stated that the company no longer needs as many customer support and operational roles because AI systems now handle the bulk of those functions. The announcement came even as Cloudflare reported record-high revenue, highlighting a sobering new dynamic: AI can simultaneously drive growth and eliminate the jobs that once sustained it.

  • 1,100 jobs cut across support and operational functions
  • CEO directly cited AI automation as the reason headcount needs shrank
  • Record revenue reported in the same quarter as the layoffs
