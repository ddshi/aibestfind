Anthropic Found a Hidden Space Where Claude Puzzles Over Concepts

In a breakthrough for AI interpretability, researchers at Anthropic have discovered a "hidden space" within their Claude model where it processes and puzzles over abstract concepts. This internal "scratchpad" offers an unprecedented glimpse into the model's reasoning process, revealing how it tackles complex problems before arriving at a final answer. The finding is a major step toward understanding the "black box" of large language models and improving their safety and reliability.

Hugging Face's CEO on Why Companies Are Done Renting Their AI

Clem Delangue, CEO of Hugging Face, argues that the corporate world is moving away from "renting" AI models from a few dominant providers and toward owning and customizing their own open-source solutions. The sentiment is a powerful counter-narrative to the "one model to rule them all" approach, suggesting a more decentralized and democratized future for enterprise AI.
