New reports indicate that Amazon CEO Andy Jassy privately expressed concerns to the board about the safety of Anthropic's models before the government's intervention. This revelation suggests that internal doubts about the technology's readiness were present at the highest levels of the company that is Anthropic's primary cloud and investment partner. The suspension of Anthropic's models has ignited a fierce debate in India about technological sovereignty and dependency on foreign AI.
