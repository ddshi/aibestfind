AI News Digest: The Week in Intelligence

Key Insights: The judicial system is facing a novel crisis: a surge of low-quality, AI-generated legal documents and lawsuits that are clogging court dockets. The trend highlights a dangerous unintended consequence of democratizing AI—the weaponization of volume against a system built on precision and relevance.
