The Infrastructure Race: Hardware, Data, and New Frontiers

Nvidia partners with data center developer Cloverleaf

Nvidia has announced a strategic partnership with data center developer Cloverleaf, signaling a move to secure more control over the physical infrastructure required to deploy its AI hardware. The collaboration is expected to focus on building specialized, high-density data centers optimized for Nvidia's GPUs and networking technology. This vertical integration strategy highlights the growing importance of co-designing hardware with the facilities that house it to meet the immense power and cooling demands of modern AI.
