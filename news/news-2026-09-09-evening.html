Mistral raises €3B as sovereign AI becomes big business

The French AI champion has closed a monumental €3 billion funding round, cementing its status as Europe's primary counterweight to American AI dominance. This investment is a clear signal that "sovereign AI"—the development of independent, regionally-controlled AI capabilities—has moved from a political talking point to a commercially viable and strategically critical business model. The round is expected to fuel Mistral's expansion into new markets and intensify its focus on developing frontier models that can operate under European regulatory frameworks.
